## Design Context

### Users

One local reader discovering books and maintaining a personal reading list; assessment reviewers need a clear, keyboard-accessible demonstration of search, save, edit, and remove.

### Brand Personality

Quiet, warm, considered. The candidate selected a quiet library feel with warm paper colors and clear typography on 2026-10-06.

### Aesthetic Direction

Light paper background, dark ink, muted rust accents, serif headings and native sans-serif controls. No external fonts/images or decorative dashboards.

### Design Principles

- Reading and actions take priority over decoration.
- Distinguish discovery from the saved collection without duplicating data.
- Preserve editable drafts and keep failures close to the affected action.
- Keep native form controls, visible focus, and narrow-screen usability.
- Keep saved books compact; reveal status/notes editors with native disclosure.
