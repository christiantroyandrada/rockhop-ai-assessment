# Design context

## Users

One local reader searching for books and keeping a personal list. Assessment
reviewers need to follow search, save, edit and remove with clear keyboard access.

## Visual direction

The candidate chose a quiet library feel on 2026-10-06: warm paper, dark ink and
muted rust, with serif headings and native sans-serif controls. No external
fonts, images or decorative dashboards.

## Principles

- Make books and actions easy to read.
- Give discovery and saved editing their own views.
- Keep drafts and show errors beside the affected action.
- Use native controls, visible focus and a layout that works on narrow screens.
- Keep saved rows compact; open the status/notes editor with native disclosure.
